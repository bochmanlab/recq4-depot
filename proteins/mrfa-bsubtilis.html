Function

Part of a nucleotide excision repair pathway specific for mitomycin C (MMC), separate from the UvrABC excision repair pathway (PubMed:30379365). Probably acts on DNA monoadducts or intra-strand cross-links induced by MMC (PubMed:30379365). Has 3'-5' DNA helicase activity (has no activity on RNA) and single-stranded (ss)DNA-dependent ATPase activity (PubMed:33300032).

Catalytic activityCouples ATP hydrolysis with the unwinding of duplex DNA by translocating in the 3'-5' direction.
ATP + H2O = ADP + phosphate + H(+)
Subunit / complexMonomer (PubMed:33300032). Interacts with MrfB (PubMed:30379365)
Domain notesssDNA binds across the surface of the RecA1 and RecA2 domains (PubMed:33300032). Upon DNA-binding the OB and CON domains reposition and enclose ssDNA, may 'ratchet' along the DNA using ATPase-driven domain repositioning (PubMed:33300032)
Disruption phenotypeIncreased sensitivity to mitomycin C (MMC) but not UV, 4-nitroquinoline 1-oxide or DNA cross-linking agent psoralen (trioxsalen) (PubMed:30379365). A double mrfA-mrfB deletion is as sensitive to MMC as each individual deletion (PubMed:30379365). Deletion of mrfAB and uvrABC makes cells hypersensitive to MMC (PubMed:30379365)
FamilyBelongs to the helicase family. HRQ1 subfamily
MiscellaneousMMC interacts with guanines to form monoadducts, intra-strand and inter-strand cross-links
