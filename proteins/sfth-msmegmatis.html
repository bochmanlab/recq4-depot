Function

An ATP-dependent 3'-5' DNA helicase, loads on a 3'-tailed substrate and unwinds DNA as it translocates in a 3'-5' direction (PubMed:22641846). The (d)ATPase activity is dependent on single-stranded (ss)DNA, only (d)ATP allows helicase activity; no other nucleotides are hydrolyzed (PubMed:22641846). Has no activity on 5'-tailed or blunt-ended DNA (PubMed:22641846).

Catalytic activityCouples ATP hydrolysis with the unwinding of duplex DNA by translocating in the 3'-5' direction.
