Function

Component of antiviral defense system Druantia type I, composed of DruA, DruB, DruC, DruD and DruE. Expression of Druantia in E.coli (strain MG1655) confers resistance to phage lambda, SECphi18, SECphi27 and T4 (PubMed:29371424). This protein is probably a helicase (Probable).

Disruption phenotypeWhen this gene is missing the Druantia system does not confer resistance to SECphi27 in E.coli
